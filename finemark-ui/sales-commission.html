📊 Commission Structure — sales commission per vehicle by bank & loan years

Each deal is paid from the cell that matches its bank and loan length (months rounded up to whole years). Payout per receipt = amount retained × (sales commission ÷ (price − bank commission)). A deal paid in full at the listed price earns exactly the listed commission, and part-payments earn a proportional share. If a deal's bank or loan length isn't in this table, the flat rate from Commission Rate Parameters applies instead. Leave all three boxes in a year empty to mean "no tier for that year".
